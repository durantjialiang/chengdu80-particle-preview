## Photo authorization

On 2026-09-10, the project owner supplied the `Hansen`, `Joel Sobel`, `Robert Anderson` and `Brady Haran` media folders and explicitly requested their biographies and event photographs on the public website, then asked for a more prominent display of these collaborators. This is the project-owner confirmation recorded by the media manifest, not a claim of an independently issued photographer licence.

Seventeen selected photographs (four per person and one shared forum group photograph) are registered in `content/collaborator-media-approved.json`. All are owner supplied and have separate approved-publication records. The selection was based on named folders, named videos, image captions/stage labels, event context and source metadata; no face recognition was used to establish identities. The source JPEGs retain their original bytes. Full WebP derivatives have a maximum long edge of 2400 px and thumbnails 800 px, preserving the complete frame and excluding source EXIF metadata. These forum and lecture photographs are kept in their own collection rather than being silently merged into competition-year albums.

The primary photographs are `DSC_7510 (1).JPG` (Hansen), `1C9A5871.JPG` (Sobel), `DSC_6342.JPG` (Anderson) and `IMG_7572 (1).JPG` (Haran). `1C9A6106.JPG` is a shared group photograph; identical copies across supplied folders are published once. ## Current identities and credentials

- **Lars Peter Hansen:** University of Chicago, David Rockefeller Distinguished Service Professor in Economics, Statistics, and the Booth School of Business; 2013 Economic Sciences Nobel co-laureate; Macro Finance Research Program director. Sources: [Chicago Booth](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/faculty/directory/h/lars-hansen), [Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2013/hansen/facts/).
- **Joel Sobel:** UC San Diego Professor Emeritus of Economics. The current departmental faculty directory takes precedence over the older active-professor title on his personal page. American Academy of Arts and Sciences member (2010), Econometric Society fellow (1990), Econometrica editor (2015–2019). Sources: [current UCSD faculty directory](https://economics.ucsd.edu/faculty-and-research/faculty-profiles/index.html), [Academy profile](https://www.amacad.org/person/joel-sobel), [UCSD CV](https://econweb.ucsd.edu/~jsobel/cv.pdf). The unrelated Journal of Econometrics label on the historical event page is not repeated.
- **Robert M. Anderson:** UC Berkeley Distinguished Professor Emeritus of Economics and Mathematics, Coleman Fung Professor Emeritus of Risk Management, Director of the Center for Risk Management Research; CDAR Co-Director. Sources: [Berkeley Economics](https://econ.berkeley.edu/profile/robert-m-anderson), [CDAR profile](https://cdar.econ.berkeley.edu/people/robert-anderson.html). Emeritus status remains explicit.
- **Brady Haran:** Numberphile creator, science communicator and independent filmmaker. His 2016 Nottingham honorary Doctor of Letters is not a faculty appointment and he is not labelled a professor. The 2024 Christopher Zeeman Medal is awarded jointly by LMS and IMA. Sources: [Numberphile](https://www.numberphile.com/about), [LMS](https://www.lms.ac.uk/news/brady-haran-2024-christopher-zeeman-medal), [Nottingham honorary degree list](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/registrar/documents/hon-deg-list-nov2021.pdf).

[SWUFE’s 2019 review](https://jinrong.swufe.edu.cn/info/1096/1404.htm) confirms that both events took place. Photos show lectures, the publishing panel, conversation and a group photograph; no judging or mentoring role is inferred.
- Anderson's supplied 2019 stage image explicitly labels his CDAR representative welcome remarks. His individual 2021 forum participation is independently documented in the [SWUFE–CDAR forum report](https://jinrong.swufe.edu.cn/info/1100/3386.htm), alongside the [fourth Chengdu 80 report](https://jinrong.swufe.edu.cn/info/1100/3387.htm). The date of the photographs remains 2019 even when the biography mentions the separate 2021 visit.
- Haran's [SWUFE “I love Numbers” lecture page](https://fife.swufe.edu.cn/info/1311/2341.htm) and the [Jiazi Innovation Forum overview](https://fife.swufe.edu.cn/info/1311/2371.htm) establish the lecture and the series’ role as a lead-in to the International FinTech Forum. [His November 2019 China-trip post](https://www.bradyharanblog.com/blog/china-trip) and the owner photo metadata support the month. The website does not invent an exact lecture day or describe him as a Chengdu 80 judge or a FinTech Forum keynote speaker.
